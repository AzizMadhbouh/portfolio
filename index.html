Ingest / Profile

Unglamorous work,
reliable results.

Most data work is janitorial. I've made peace with that — and gotten good at it.

I'm in my final year of a Big Data & Data Analysis degree at ISAMM. My projects usually start with a file nobody wants to open: 4.1 million purchase rows, 110,000 appointment records, a pile of Jenkins logs.

The interesting part is what comes after. That means distributed processing on Spark and Hadoop, a database that can take the load, and a model whose limits I can explain without a slide deck.

I care about the things that make a demo survive contact with a real system: tests, migrations, reproducible containers, and logs that tell you what actually broke.
